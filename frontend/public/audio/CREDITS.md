- File: `can-pop.mp3`

- Downloaded public high-quality preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/523/523322_2384251-hq.mp3

Adapted for Water Together: trimmed to 0.28–1.53 seconds, removed low rumble
with a 65 Hz high-pass filter, reduced level by 4 dB, limited peaks to 0.6,
added a 6 ms fade-in
and 200 ms fade-out. Encoded as 44.1 kHz stereo, 128 kbps MP3.
No synthetic oscillator tones are mixed into the recording.

The sound is hosted with the app and precached for offline foreground playback.
Background / locked-screen Web Push uses the operating system's notification
sound; a web app cannot replace that sound with this recording.
